FAQ

Is this a physical product?

No. It's a digital download you can print or use on a tablet.

Does it follow the US school year?

No. It covers the 2027 calendar year (January to December) with dated weekly pages. It is not a 2027-28 August-to-July planner.

Do I need a paid AI tool?

No. You can use the prompts with the free versions of ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude (they have daily usage limits).

Can I print it more than once?

Yes, as many times as you want for personal use.

What size is it?

US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). To print on A4, choose “Fit to page” in your print settings.
